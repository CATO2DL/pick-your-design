# Reference decisions

Viewed on 2026-10-26. These are real source websites, not generated candidates. Website content can change; descriptions record the inspected view. Local screenshots were displayed in two boards and checked for successful image loading. They are not redistributed because no screenshot redistribution license was established. The URLs and concrete observations below make the design decision portable without embedding those screenshots.

| ID | Source | Observed distinction | Decision |

| B | [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/) | Blue service masthead, strong text hierarchy, prominent functional links. | Not selected. |

| G | [Daunt Books](https://dauntbooks.co.uk/) | Serif masthead above a broad bookstore-interior photograph. | Round-two comparison for photographic presentation. |
| H | [Kinfolk](https://www.kinfolk.com/) | Centered serif title, small magazine-cover image, generous white space. | Round-two comparison for image scale and whitespace. |
| I | [Daunt Books Publishing — About](https://dauntbookspublishing.co.uk/about/) | Text-led serif paragraphs with little image emphasis. | Deliberately different round-two direction. |

Round one displayed A–F. A person chose F. Round two displayed the unchanged F anchor plus new G–I; IDs were not reassigned. The person then specifically confirmed **F's large photography and centered copy**. This is a narrow confirmation, not approval of F's brand, palette, exact font, products, or all interactions.

Borrow only the general compositional relationship: a photograph occupies most of the hero, with concise centered copy placed over it. Do not copy Aesop's logo, product imagery, copy, exact styling, or commercial identity. Use original text, system fonts, and the separately licensed photograph documented in [asset notes](site/assets/README.md).

**Suggested, not confirmed:** warm neutral base, serif headings, one-page structure, component sizes, green accent after a controlled revision, and all fictional concept copy. No inferred brand facts are presented as user requirements.
